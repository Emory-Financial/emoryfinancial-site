Historic homes and the appraisal problem

Bungalow Heaven, Madison Heights, Garfield Heights — Pasadena's older neighborhoods are the draw and the complication. Unpermitted additions, converted basements and square footage that disagrees with county records all show up at appraisal, and any of them can reduce your loan amount late in escrow. Worth flagging early on any pre-1950 property.

Landmark districts and insurance

Homes in landmark or historic districts can carry restrictions that affect replacement-cost insurance, and lenders require adequate coverage before they'll fund. It's rarely a deal-breaker, but it's a step that surprises people when it appears three days before closing.

Condo warrantability near Old Town and along the Gold Line

There's a lot of attached housing along the Metro corridor. Financing depends on the HOA's reserves, owner-occupancy ratio and litigation status — not just your credit. I check the building before you commit to it.

Jumbo is common here

With the county limit at $1,249,125, plenty of Pasadena purchases run above conforming. Jumbo guidelines are stricter but the pricing is frequently competitive, and portfolio lenders have flexibility that agency guidelines don't.
