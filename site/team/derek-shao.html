How I work

I still take every file myself. When you call, I answer — no call center, no handoff to a processor you've never met.

Being independent means I'm not selling one bank's shelf. I shop the wholesale market across many lenders, compare the real pricing side by side, and place your loan where it genuinely fits. When one lender's guidelines don't work, I move the file rather than telling you no.

What I specialize in

  • Self-employed and business-owner borrowers whose tax returns understate their income
  • Jumbo and high-balance purchases across Los Angeles and Orange County
  • Multigenerational and family-money purchases, including co-signer situations
  • Deciding between a cash-out refinance, a HELOC and a fixed second lien
  • Condos with HOA complications, including pending litigation
