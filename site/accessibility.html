Accessibility Statement

Emory Financial is committed to making this website usable by everyone. Last updated July 27, 2026.

Our commitment

We want every visitor to be able to use this site, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification, or other assistive technology. Housing and lending should be accessible to everyone, and that starts with the website.

What we've done

  • Semantic HTML structure with proper headings and landmarks
  • A skip-to-content link and full keyboard navigation with visible focus indicators
  • Text and background color combinations chosen to meet contrast guidelines
  • Labels on every form field and descriptive text for meaningful images and charts
  • Responsive layout that works when text is enlarged or on small screens
  • Animation reduced automatically when your device requests reduced motion

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA.

Ongoing work

Accessibility isn't a one-time project. We review the site periodically and fix issues as we find them. Some third-party content we link to may not meet the same standard, and we don't control those sites.

Tell us about a problem

If you run into a barrier on this site, please tell us — it's the fastest way for us to fix it. Describe the page and what happened, and we'll respond and work with you to get you the information you need by another route in the meantime.
